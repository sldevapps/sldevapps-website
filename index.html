How we build.

A few principles show up in everything we make.

Privacy by design

Your data is yours. We keep processing on the device whenever we can and are plain about what leaves it and why.

Useful before clever

We solve everyday problems well: fewer taps, clear screens, features that behave the same way every time.

Built with care

Careful testing, deliberate changes and attention to accessibility, so apps stay dependable as they grow.
